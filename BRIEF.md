# Scalia · BRIEF (first draft)

Self-authored from the client's master prompt ("PROMPT MAÎTRE"), which fixes all copy, palette, typography,
section order and behaviours. No interview was run: the prompt is the brief.

- Vibe: contemporary editorial luxury, architecture of connection, invisible technology.
- Journey: visual impact → understanding → credibility → expertise → offer → price → appointment.
- Energy: hero (calm, cinematic) → credibility (quiet, reading) → optimisations (peak) → infrastructure (low, dark, steady) → pricing (clear) → identity (soft) → rendez-vous (warm) → final (strong, dark).
- Grammar: editorial chapters, alternating light/dark. Not a filmic one-shot: the client asked for a looping hero film, not a scrubbed flight.
- Signature move: the champagne connection line. It opens from the centre under the navbar, seams the ivory sheet onto the film,
  fills the credibility spine, and peaks when six orthogonal wires connect the Scalia symbol to each optimisation axis.
- Peak: "It's the site where the logo literally wires itself to everything it promises." (Optimisations, desktop, largest pinned span.)
- Assets: client logo only. Hero videos, posters, Calendly URL and contact details are placeholders.
